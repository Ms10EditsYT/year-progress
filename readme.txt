# Year Progress Bar

A simple and modern year progress bar that dynamically shows how much of the current year has passed. It also displays the remaining days in the year.

## Features
- **Real-time progress bar**: Shows how much of the year has passed.
- **Dynamic date updates**: The date and time are updated live, reflecting the user's timezone and language.
- **Quarter markers**: Visual indicators for each quarter of the year (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4).
- **User's local timezone**: Automatically adjusts to the user's time zone and language.

## How It Works
The progress bar shows the percentage of the year that has passed, based on the current date and time. The quarter markers (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4) are placed according to the actual end of each quarter, and the remaining days in the year are displayed.

### Technologies Used:
- **HTML**: Markup for the structure of the page.
- **CSS**: Styling for the progress bar and layout.
- **JavaScript**: Logic for updating the progress bar and displaying the date and time.
